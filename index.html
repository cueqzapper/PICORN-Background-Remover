WHY PICORN

A small model, plus image-aware correction.

01

One compact alpha model

A 524k-parameter ONNX graph predicts a soft foreground matte instead of a hard cutout.

02

Edges that follow the image

Eight small WebGPU passes follow similar colours. A guided pass cleans the alpha without erasing hair, wires or small holes.

03

Hints that understand regions

The refine brush builds a local trimap, grows through connected pixels and keeps changes inside a controlled band.
